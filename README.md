# Super-Store-Data-Project

### Project Overview

This Super Store data analysis project aims to provide insights into the store’s sales and profit performance over the past year by examining key areas such as customer segments, product categories, regions, and shipping modes, with the goal of identifying trends, uncovering opportunities, and supporting data-driven business decisions while gaining a clearer understanding of overall store performance.

### Data Source

Super store Data: The primary dataset used for this analysis is the "superstore_data.csv" file, containing detailed information about each sale made by the store.

### Tools

Power BI-With the help of power BI,i was able to extract,transform(ETL) and load the data.

### Data extraction

This is the first step that must be taken, i extracted the store data from it source.

### Data Cleaning

The data was cleaned in Power BI using Power Query to make sure everything was consistent and ready for analysis. Missing values and duplicates were checked, data types were corrected, and date fields were formatted properly for time-based analysis. Sales and profit values were reviewed for accuracy, and categories such as product type, customer segment, shipping mode, and region were standardized. This ensured the dashboard results were clear and reliable.

### Load data

The cleaned data was loaded, and a quick check was done to make sure everything came in correctly. Once confirmed, the data was ready to be used for visualization.

### Data visualization

After loading the super store data, I used DAX formulas to calculate key metrics such as total sales, total profit, average delivery days, and total customers. I also explored how profit changes over time and across different categories, sub-categories, states, customer segments, and shipping modes.

To make the insights easy to understand, I created interactive charts that highlight sales trends, profitability, and customer behavior. ### Result/Finding

The analysis shows that the Super Store recorded strong overall sales and healthy profitability, with certain years performing significantly better than others. Technology products contributed the highest share of profit, while furniture generated comparatively lower returns.

Customer purchases were largely driven by the consumer segment, and standard class shipping proved to be the most profitable delivery option. Profitability also varied by location, with a few states standing out as top performers. Overall, the findings highlight clear opportunities to focus on high-performing categories, optimize shipping methods, and strengthen performance in underperforming regions.

### Recommendations

Based on the analysis,it’s clear that some areas perform better than others. Paying attention to these differences can help guide future decisions.
